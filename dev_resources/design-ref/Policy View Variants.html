Design notes. All three variants reuse the existing system — Geist family, slate-50 canvas, rounded-2xl white cards with the subtle shad-card shadow, slate-900 primary buttons, Geist Mono for IDs/timestamps, and the established status-pill set (emerald · amber · violet · rose). They differ in where the policy body sits relative to its metadata and engagement — document, workspace, or hero — so you can pick the framing that matches how your team actually reads policies.
